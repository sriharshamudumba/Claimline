Call in a claim and watch the file fill in.

Claimline is a voice agent for commercial claims: trucking, cyber, and liability. Pick who you're calling as and talk normally. While you talk, it builds what an adjuster would get: deadlines, coverage questions, severity, and where the claim is routed. People still make every coverage decision.

Use headphones so the agent doesn't hear itself. Calls end after 6 minutes.

Or listen to a recorded call

No microphone needed. The script is fixed, but every step runs through the real claims engine.

    Every person, company, policy, and claim in these calls is made up. Claimline is a personal demo and isn't affiliated with any insurer.
